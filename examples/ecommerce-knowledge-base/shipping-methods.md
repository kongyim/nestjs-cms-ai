# Shipping methods

UK standard delivery costs GBP 4.95 and takes 3 to 5 business days. Standard UK delivery is free for orders of GBP 60 or more after discounts. UK express delivery costs GBP 8.95 and takes 1 to 2 business days. Orders placed before 14:00 UK time on a business day are dispatched that day if all items are in stock.
