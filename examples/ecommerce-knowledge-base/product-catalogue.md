# Product catalogue

The fictional Northstar Supply catalogue includes the Aurora desk lamp with a two-year warranty, the Trail bottle with a one-year warranty, and the Orbit USB-C hub with a two-year warranty. The Aurora lamp costs GBP 49, the Trail bottle GBP 24, and the Orbit hub GBP 69. The Trail bottle holds 750 ml. The Orbit hub supports HDMI output up to 4K at 60 Hz.
